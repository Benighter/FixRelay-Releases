# FixRelay 0.1.0

- Send screenshots and fix instructions from Android to Codex on Windows.
- Share images/text from another Android app or attach them in a report.
- Keep submitted reports online while your PC is off; resume pickup after Windows sign-in and reconnection.
- View report history, progress, saved chat identity, and results.
- Pair phones with one-time codes; pause processing or revoke a phone from the PC.
- Check for signed Android updates from Settings.

Windows coding work is isolated for review. FixRelay does not automatically merge, publish, or release app changes. Approvals and interrupted jobs require attention on the PC. Initial verification used a 64-bit Android emulator; physical-phone testing remains separate.
